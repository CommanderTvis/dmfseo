# Original data findings — rank tracker, 31 Oct to 11 Nov 2026

Published on the site as the data report in the SEO / GEO Lab ([EN](https://business.constructor.university/guillemots/grohnpetrel-seo-aeo-geo-lab/), [DE](https://business.constructor.university/guillemots/de/seo-geo-labor/)).

## Data and method

- Source: `gh-pages:data/results.csv` (raw, one row per check), written by `scripts/collect.py`.
- Google via the Serper.dev API: `hl=en`, `gl=de|us|gb`, organic top 10, data-centre IP (not personalised, no city).
- Derived table: Google rows only; for each date, query and country the **last** check of the day (same rule as the dashboard). Rank = position of our best page; blank = not in the returned results.
- Reproduce: `git show origin/gh-pages:data/results.csv > results.csv && uv run scripts/rank_report.py results.csv --until 2026-11-11 --png chart.png --csv daily.csv`
- Chart: `media/grohnpetrel-rank-chart-2026-11-11.webp` (generated by the same script).

## Interpretation (hypotheses only)

H1 ambiguity of a new word with several meanings; H2 exact-match stability of the quoted query; H3 coincidence of gaps (3 and 5 Nov) with large site changes; H4 social posts/videos at the top for the bare query in the UK/US, which organic rank does not count. Each hypothesis is stated with what would contradict it on the Lab page. No causal claim is made.

## Limitations

12 days; one API; one interface language; three countries; no volume data; organic results only (no AI Overviews, videos or images); other teams' changes are not observed in detail.
