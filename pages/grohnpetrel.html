About the project

What Is Grohnpetrel?

Inspired by Grohn, the Bremen district on the banks of the Weser where Constructor University is located, and by the biodiversity of the nearby North Sea and Wadden Sea coast, our goal is to raise awareness among young people and adults about the challenges facing coastal ecosystems and show how small actions that may seem insignificant can help protect them.

The word Grohnpetrel is also used by other, unrelated websites, for example for drinks or clothing. On this site it always means our academic coastal-conservation and seabird-education concept.

What we aim for

Our Main Goals

1

Protect coastal ecosystems

Raise awareness and help people understand the problems affecting our coasts, and make them aware of how pollution, plastic waste, and the loss of natural habitats directly impact them and in what ways.

2

Educate about seabirds

Teach about the different species of seabirds, their importance to ecosystems, and the threats they face, including those related to human activities.

3

Encourage community participation

Motivate students and other people interested in the environment to adopt responsible habits and learn about the different ways they can contribute to coastal conservation.

Why it matters

Why Coastal Conservation Matters

Beaches and coasts are home to many species of animals and plants, and they play an important role in the balance of marine ecosystems.

However, everyday human activities have negative effects like pollution, waste buildup, and the alteration of natural habitats, which pose threats to these environments. Seabirds can also be affected by these problems, as they rely on ocean and coastal conditions to feed and reproduce.

At Grohnpetrel, we believe that understanding these challenges is one of the first steps to grasping the importance of protecting our ecosystems.

Quick answers

Grohnpetrel Questions, Answered

Is Grohnpetrel a real organisation?

No. Grohnpetrel is a fictional academic concept. It is not a registered charity or nonprofit, it does not carry out conservation work and it does not accept donations.

Is Grohnpetrel a real bird?

No. Grohnpetrel is not a scientifically recognised species. The name was invented for this project and is inspired by petrels, real seabirds that spend most of their lives at sea.

Who made Grohnpetrel?

Grohnpetrel was created in autumn 2026 by the Guillemots, a team of four students at Constructor University in Bremen, for the course Digital Marketing Fundamentals and Search Engine Optimisation. It is not an official university initiative. More answers in the Grohnpetrel FAQ.
