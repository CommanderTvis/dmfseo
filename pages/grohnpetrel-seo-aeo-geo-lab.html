How the Guillemots team measures whether an invented term, Grohnpetrel, becomes discoverable in search engines and AI assistants, and the evidence we have collected so far.

Research status: this page reports only observations we have actually made, with dates. We do not claim rankings, impressions, backlinks or AI citations that we have not measured.

Research question

Can a new, invented term such as “Grohnpetrel” become findable, correctly understood and cited across traditional search (SEO), answer engines (AEO) and generative AI assistants (GEO), and which actions make a measurable difference?

What we measure

Fixed test prompts

  • Grohnpetrel
  • What is Grohnpetrel?
  • Is Grohnpetrel a real bird species?
  • What is the Grohnpetrel initiative?
  • What does Grohnpetrel aim to achieve?
  • Who created Grohnpetrel?

Method: hypothesis, action, observation

  1. Record a baseline before publishing anything.
  2. Publish useful core pages: a clear definition, mission, programmes, sourced guides and FAQ, with internal links.
  3. Check technical discovery: response headers, sitemap, indexability and Search Console.
  4. Test controlled changes and note the dates.
  5. Compare and reflect: separate measured changes from interpretations, and compare fairly with other teams.

Baseline evidence (before publication)

Before any page existed, all three search engines and all five AI answer tools we tested either did not recognise “Grohnpetrel” or treated it as a misspelling of real seabird names such as the giant petrel or grey petrel.

Technical finding: the server told search engines not to index

On 28 October 2026, every URL we tested on business.constructor.university returned the header X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow. According to Google’s documentation, this keeps pages out of search results. The header is set on the server, outside WordPress. Status: resolved; by 31 October Google was ranking our pages, and on 1 November our own check confirmed the header was gone. More in our Journal article What a Hidden Noindex Header Taught Us About SEO.

Results so far: Google rankings

Only our front page ranked on these dates. Another team’s site (Puffins) held #1 for “grohnpetrel” in the US and Germany. Brave and DuckDuckGo did not show our site in the top 10. Search Console had no data yet.

What we changed, and when

Each change is also recorded with its outcome in our project log. We will add dated results for each change here, including changes that show no effect.

What we expect, and what would prove us wrong

  • Hypothesis 1: a dedicated, answer-first definition page will be the page Google shows for “What is Grohnpetrel?”.
  • Hypothesis 2: clear, consistent facts across our pages and in structured data (who, what, where, status) will reduce the “misspelling” interpretations in AI answers.
  • Hypothesis 3: being indexed by Bing will matter for ChatGPT and Copilot visibility, not only Google indexing.
