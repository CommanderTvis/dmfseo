In brief: This guide introduces six seabirds and coastal birds of the North Sea region, from the gannet, with wings up to 1.8 m across, to the storm petrel, just 14–18 cm long. Two of them, the Atlantic puffin and the black-legged kittiwake, are classed as Vulnerable worldwide. Every fact links to its source.

Six birds at a glance

Where to see seabirds in Germany

Helgoland, an island in the German Bight, has Germany’s only bird cliff, the Lummenfelsen. About 10,000 pairs nest there, including common guillemots, northern gannets, black-legged kittiwakes, razorbills and northern fulmars (Helgoland tourism office). Along the mainland coast, the Wadden Sea, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2009, is a key area for shorebirds such as the oystercatcher and for nesting terns.

What threatens these birds

The same pressures come up again and again: less food as small fish such as sandeels decline, climate change, bycatch in fishing gear, introduced predators on nesting islands, pollution, disturbance of nests and, since 2022, bird flu. Read more in Seabird Conservation: Threats and Challenges.
