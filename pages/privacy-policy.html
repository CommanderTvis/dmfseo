The website runs on a WordPress server at business.constructor.university, operated by Constructor University. 3. Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or tracking tools, so it shows no cookie banner.

4. Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to see how our pages appear in Google Search. It works with aggregated search data and sets no cookies on this website.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them when the project ends.
