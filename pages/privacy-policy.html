2. Hosting and server log files

The website runs on a WordPress server at business.constructor.university, operated by Constructor University. When you visit a page, the server may record technical data such as your IP address, date and time, the page requested and your browser type. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use this data to identify you.

3. Cookies and consent

We use the Complianz plugin to ask for your consent. A functional cookie stores your choice so the banner does not appear again (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; § 25(2) TDDDG). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the Cookie Policy. Browsers that send a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal are treated as if you had declined.

4. Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click “Accept”, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, to measure how many people visit our pages and where they come from (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; § 25(1) TDDDG). Google Analytics sets cookies and may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. If you click “Deny”, Google Analytics is not loaded.

5. Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to see how our pages appear in Google Search. It works with aggregated search data and sets no cookies on this website.

6. Contact by email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them when the project ends.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to data portability, and to object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To use these rights, email us at mirahetalopez@constructor.university.
