In brief: We do not sell anything. Grohnpetrel merchandise is a plan for the future, made for our course, and nothing can be ordered on this website. If the project ever became a registered organisation, any income would go to education, and we would say exactly how.

What we are planning

Each planned item has a job in the range and carries a QR “learning link” to a page with sourced information, so the product teaches before it sells.

Our rules for merchandise

  • No sales before registration: no money is taken while Grohnpetrel is a fictional student project.
  • Accuracy first: every fact printed on a product must also appear on this website with its source.
  • Learning before selling: products never replace the educational content of this site.
  • Honest promises: if items are ever made to order, delivery times and the use of any income will be stated clearly.

Why a merchandise plan?

Our course asks each team to plan how it could sell Grohnpetrel merchandise in the future and to justify that plan to a CEO. This page shows the public side of our plan; the full marketing plan is part of our course presentation.
