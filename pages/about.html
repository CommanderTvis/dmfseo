In brief: Grohnpetrel was created by the Guillemots, a team of four students at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany, for the course Digital Marketing Fundamentals and Search Engine Optimisation (Fall 2026). It is a fictional academic proposal: not a registered NGO, not an official university initiative, and it does not accept donations.

Who we are

Why we created Grohnpetrel

Our course asked every team to compete for search visibility for the same invented keyword, “Grohnpetrel”, and to decide what it means. We chose a coastal-conservation and seabird-education concept because it lets us publish information that is useful and sourced, not text written only for search engines.

The name joins Grohn, the Bremen district where our campus is, with petrel, a group of seabirds. Read the full definition on What is Grohnpetrel?

How we work

  • We plan our search and AI-visibility work in steps and record every action, its date, reason and outcome in a project log.
  • We measure results with Google Search Console, a daily rank check and dated tests in AI assistants.
  • We only state facts we can source, and we do not claim projects, partners, donations or impact that do not exist.

Our use of AI

We used an AI assistant to help with planning, first drafts and technical checks of this website. Every published text was reviewed and edited by our team, and all decisions are ours.
