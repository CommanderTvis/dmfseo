In brief: This page explains how the Guillemots team writes, checks and corrects the content of this website, and lists every correction we have made. Grohnpetrel is a fictional academic project: it is not a registered organisation, carries out no conservation work and takes no donations.

Who writes this website

The four students of Team Guillemots at Constructor University in Bremen: Ayoyemi Adeboyeyo, Diego Aguilar, Mario Iraheta and Iaroslav Postovalov (about the team). Every page shows when it was published and last changed.

How we choose and check sources

  • We prefer primary and official sources: public agencies (for example the German Environment Agency), international bodies (UNESCO, OSPAR, the IUCN Red List), BirdLife International and peer-reviewed studies. We use Wikipedia only for general background, and say so.
  • Every page with facts lists its sources, usually in a “Sources” section at the end.
  • Before publishing an article we check each number against its source; the date of that check is stated on the article where we have it.
  • We describe our own data (rankings, Search Console figures) with the method and its limits, and we do not present hypotheses as results.

How we use AI

We use an AI assistant for planning, first drafts, translations, technical checks and our short videos. Every published text is reviewed and edited by the team, and all decisions are ours. Translated pages say that they were translated with AI help and that the English version is the original.

What we never do

  • Claim partners, experts, reviews, donations, volunteers or conservation results that do not exist.
  • Present Grohnpetrel as a real organisation, a charity or a bird species. The “fictional project, no donations” note appears on the main pages in every language.
  • Buy or exchange links, or publish texts written only for search engines.
  • Use images without permission: we use our own graphics or openly licensed photos, credited on the page.

How to report an error

If you find a mistake, please tell us through our contact page. We check the source, correct the page if needed and record the change below. Every change to the website is also recorded in our public project log and its version history.

Corrections log

No error in the published facts about birds or places has been reported or found so far. If one is, it will be listed here with the date and the correction.
