In brief: “Grohnpetrel” is an invented word, so it has no dictionary meaning. In autumn 2026 several student teams in the same university course were each given the word and asked to define it and make it findable. That is why you will find different “Grohnpetrels” online, for example drinks or rain-gear brand concepts. On this website, Grohnpetrel means a fictional coastal-conservation and seabird-education initiative from Bremen-Grohn, created by the Guillemots team.

Where the word comes from

The course Digital Marketing Fundamentals and Search Engine Optimisation at Constructor University in Bremen (Fall 2026) gave every student team the same invented keyword, “Grohnpetrel”. Each team had to decide what the word means, build a website around that meaning and try to rank for it in search engines and AI answers. None of the meanings is “official”: they are parallel student projects. The word itself joins Grohn, the Bremen district where the university is, with petrel, a group of seabirds.

The different meanings you may find

The list describes what we observed on public websites in November 2026; other teams may change their concepts. We do not link to or rate other teams’ projects.

How to tell which Grohnpetrel a page is about

  • Look at the subject: seabirds, coasts and conservation education point to this project; products and prices point to a brand concept.
  • Look for the team name: this project is by the Guillemots (Ayoyemi Adeboyeyo, Diego Aguilar, Mario Iraheta, Iaroslav Postovalov).
  • Look at the address: this project lives at business.constructor.university/guillemots/.
  • All versions are student coursework: none is a real company, registered charity or bird species.
