Welcome to the Grohnpetrel Knowledge Center!

This is a space dedicated to gathering educational information about seabirds, coastal ecosystems, and their conservation. Our goal is to make resources accessible that help better understand the environmental issues affecting our coasts and discover different ways to contribute to their protection.

Explore Our Resources!

In this section, you can find our guides, recommendations, and other informative materials related to marine biodiversity. We want this space to be a starting point for students, nature lovers, and anyone interested in learning more about the environment.

1. Seabird Conservation: Threats and Challenges

Discover the main dangers that seabirds face! These includes plastic pollution, fishing activities, climate change, and the destruction of their habitats.

In this guide, we explain how these problems affect birds and why their conservation is important for marine ecosystems.

2. Take Action: How to Help Protect Seabirds

Learn about different actions you can incorporate into your daily life to help protect coasts and seabirds.

You'll find recommendations on reducing waste, respecting wildlife, and responsible ways to enjoy natural spaces.

3. Grohnpetrel Programmes

Explore the three educational programs we propose to develop at Grohnpetrel: Coastal Classroom, Citizen Observation, and Responsible Coast.

Discover their goals, the activities they would include, and how they would aim to promote environmental education and community involvement.

4. Seabirds of the North Sea

Meet six real seabirds and shorebirds of the North Sea region, from the northern gannet on Helgoland to the tiny European storm petrel. Each page gives size, food, status and threats, with sources. Open the seabird guide.

5. In Depth: How Plastic Pollution Affects North Sea Seabirds

What is measured on the North Sea coast: plastic in fulmar stomachs, gannet nests built with fishing nets on Helgoland, and the global picture, with all sources.

6. In Depth: Why the Wadden Sea Matters for Birds

The UNESCO World Heritage Wadden Sea in numbers: 10 to 12 million migratory birds a year, which species are increasing or declining, and how to visit without disturbing birds.

7. Seabird and Coast Glossary

Short definitions of the terms used on this site, from petrel and bird cliff to Wadden Sea, ghost nets and microplastics.

8. Download: Six North Sea Seabirds, a Student Learning Guide (PDF)

A printable four-page guide: the six birds at a glance, two sourced observations per bird, learning questions, a short glossary and all sources. Free to use for learning; photos credited under CC BY-SA 4.0.
