Protecting seabirds and coastal ecosystems is not just the responsibility of scientists or environmental organizations. The decisions we make when visiting a beach, using plastic products, or interacting with nature can also have consequences for these environments.

Even though our actions often seem small, adopting more responsible habits can help reduce some of the problems that seabirds face. Here are several recommendations that anyone can consider helping protect them.

Simple Ways to Help Protect Seabirds

Reduce Plastic Waste

One of the easiest ways to help protect the coasts is to reduce the amount of waste we generate. Using reusable bottles, avoiding unnecessary plastic products, and disposing of trash in the proper places are habits we can incorporate into our daily lives.

Why it matters: how plastic pollution affects North Sea seabirds.

When we visit a beach, it’s also important to pick up our waste and prevent it from ending up in the ocean, where it could harm birds and other marine animals.

Respect Wildlife and Nesting Areas

Watching seabirds in their natural habitat can be an interesting experience, but we should do it without interfering with their behavior. Getting too close, making loud noises, or trying to touch their nests can cause stress and disrupt their activities.

That's why it's important to keep a proper distance, respect protected areas, and follow the guidelines of local environmental authorities.

Before your next visit: how to visit the coast responsibly, with the rules of the Wadden Sea National Park and why dogs on a lead matter most.

Avoid Feeding Wild Seabirds

Although feeding birds might seem like a kind gesture, doing so can disrupt their natural behavior and make them rely on humans. Additionally, some foods we eat are not suitable for them. It's best to let birds find their own food and watch them without interfering. This way, we can enjoy their presence without unnecessarily affecting their habits.

Learn and Share Environmental Information

Education can also help with conservation. Learning about seabirds, understanding the challenges they face, and sharing reliable information with others helps raise environmental awareness. Schools, universities, and communities can get involved through educational activities, discussions, and small initiatives that promote respect for coastal ecosystems.

Organizations Working to Protect Birds and Coastal Habitats

BirdLife International

BirdLife International is an international organization dedicated to the protection of birds and their habitats. Through a network of partner organizations in different countries, it works to conserve threatened species, protect ecosystems, and promote the importance of biodiversity.

NABU

NABU (Nature and Biodiversity Conservation Union) is a German environmental organization that works to protect nature and biodiversity. Its activities include bird conservation, the protection of natural habitats, and environmental education. It is a go-to source for those who want to learn more about conservation efforts in Germany.

Wadden Sea National Parks

The national parks of the Wadden Sea protect one of the most important coastal ecosystems in Europe. Their official sites offer information about the region's nature, the birds that live there, and the rules visitors must follow to avoid harming the protected ecosystems.
