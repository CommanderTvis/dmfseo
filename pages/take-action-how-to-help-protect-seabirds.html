Protecting seabirds and coastal ecosystems is not just the responsibility of scientists or environmental organizations. The decisions we make when visiting a beach, using plastic products, or interacting with nature can also have consequences for these environments.

Even though our actions often seem small, adopting more responsible habits can help reduce some of the problems that seabirds face. Here are several recommendations that anyone can consider helping protect them.

5 Ways to Protect Seabirds and Coastal Habitats

Five things anyone can do, each based on evidence we cite on this site. Grohnpetrel is a student project and does no rescue or field work, so these are actions for you.

  1. Reduce plastic waste Use reusable bottles and bags, avoid unnecessary single-use plastic and put rubbish in a bin, also on the beach. On the German North Sea coast, 48% of fulmars found had more than 0.1 g of plastic in their stomachs. The evidence.
  2. Keep your distance from nests and colonies Watch seabirds without changing what they do: getting too close, loud noise or touching nests causes stress and can leave eggs unprotected. If birds call loudly or dive at you, you are too close. How to visit the coast responsibly.
  3. Keep dogs on a lead and stay on marked paths In a study of nesting plovers, walkers with dogs flushed the birds from their nests in 80% of cases on paths, walkers without dogs in 12.9%. In the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park dogs must be on a lead all year in the quiet and intermediate zones. Why it matters.
  4. Do not feed wild seabirds Feeding may seem kind, but it changes the birds’ natural behaviour, makes them rely on people, and much of our food is not suitable for them. Let birds find their own food and enjoy watching them.
  5. Learn, share and join established initiatives Share reliable, sourced information with friends, classes and clubs, support the organisations listed below, and join an established clean-up such as the International Coastal Cleanup Day, organised in Germany by NABU. Organizations Working to Protect Birds and Coastal Habitats

BirdLife International

BirdLife International is an international organization dedicated to the protection of birds and their habitats. Through a network of partner organizations in different countries, it works to conserve threatened species, protect ecosystems, and promote the importance of biodiversity.

NABU

NABU (Nature and Biodiversity Conservation Union) is a German environmental organization that works to protect nature and biodiversity. Its activities include bird conservation, the protection of natural habitats, and environmental education. It is a go-to source for those who want to learn more about conservation efforts in Germany.

Wadden Sea National Parks

The national parks of the Wadden Sea protect one of the most important coastal ecosystems in Europe. Their official sites offer information about the region's nature, the birds that live there, and the rules visitors must follow to avoid harming the protected ecosystems.
